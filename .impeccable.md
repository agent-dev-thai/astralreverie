## Design Context

### Users

Gacha-literate players and curious spectators using a phone or desktop browser for a short, consequence-free pull session. They want the anticipation, reveal spectacle, collection progress, and luck audit of a modern character gacha without real purchases.

### Brand Personality

Cosmic, theatrical, and knowingly predatory. The interface should deliver premium space-opera drama while its copy stays dry, self-aware, and honest that every currency and purchase is simulated.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dark, cinematic one-page pull terminal with original space-fantasy characters, editorial asymmetry, warm astral gold, ember coral, and deep plum-black neutrals. It may evoke the production value and reveal drama of contemporary space-opera gachas, but it must not copy Honkai: Star Rail characters, logos, names, composition, or UI chrome. Avoid generic cyan-on-dark dashboards, purple-blue gradient text, glass-card grids, and placeholder monograms where real art is available.

### Design Principles

1. Make the pull the unmistakable focal action; supporting information should orbit it.
2. Build anticipation through pacing, sound, motion, and rarity color—not visual clutter.
3. Keep odds, pity, fake spending, and history legible so spectacle never hides the rules.
4. Use satire as a pressure valve: premium presentation, plainly fake commerce.
5. Adapt the composition for mobile and desktop instead of merely scaling a phone frame.
