# Community code of conduct

Astral Reverie welcomes constructive contributions from people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and generous in review.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect creative ownership, privacy, and attribution.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and safe for participation.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report urgent abuse using GitHub's reporting tools or contact an Agent Dev Thai organization owner privately; do not post sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.
